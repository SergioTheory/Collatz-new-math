# Collatz Crystal Hunter: A Computational Map and Large-Deviation Theory

This repository contains the complete theoretical, computational, and formal verification package for the Collatz Crystal Hunter research programme. The research is divided into two companion papers: **Part I** (computational mapping of the space) and **Part II** (large-deviation theory and exact transport).

## Repository Structure

- **papers/**: Contains the LaTeX source and compiled PDFs of the two main papers.
  - part1_computational_map.tex / .pdf: The first paper detailing the discovery of Zone 2, the archipelago of confluence centers, the dead zone, and constructive obstructions.
  - part2_large_deviation_theory.tex / .pdf: The second paper establishing the exact 2-adic finite-scale transport, the boundary-layer Fourier cancellation, and the resolution of the Hausdorff dimension bound.
- **scripts/**: The computational engine of the project. Contains highly optimized, multiprocessed Python scripts for verifying the stationary geometric decay, parity scanning, soliton dominance, and Fourier spectral analysis. These scripts are the verification artifacts for Part I.
- **data/**: Datasets (JSON, CSV), logs, and figures generated by the computational experiments, including the stationary phase survival tracking across scales up to =32$ and confluence center censuses.
- **lean/**: The Lean 4 formalization of the exact conditional transport (Module 1) and the structural recurrences bypassing total variation (Module 2), accompanying Part II. Built as a standard Lean library.
- **supplementary/**: Full tables and additional computational logs.
- **rchive/**: Obsolete and early-stage Lean sketches.
